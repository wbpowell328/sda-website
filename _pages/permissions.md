This website is designed as a public resource. You may use any of this material for your teaching without attribution, regardless of whether you are copying material directly into a lecture, or blending the information into your existing teaching material.

If you are using material in a publication that you feel should be attributed to the website, use the guidance below.

## How to cite

Which citation to use depends on what you are referring to — a specific webpage, the site as a whole, or one of the books that is also hosted here (in which case cite the book, not the page).

### A specific webpage

> Powell, W. B. (2026). *How to teach decision framing*. warrenpowell.org. <https://warrenpowell.org/teaching-framing/> (accessed *date*).

Substitute the page's own title, URL, and the date you accessed it.

### The site as a whole

> Powell, W. B. (2026). *warrenpowell.org*. <https://warrenpowell.org>

### A book hosted on the site

Cite the book itself; the online reader is just one way to access it.

> Powell, W. B. (2022). *Reinforcement Learning and Stochastic Optimization: A unified framework for sequential decisions*. John Wiley & Sons.

> Powell, W. B. (2026). *Sequential Decision Analytics and Modeling* (2nd ed.). Kindle Direct Publishing. <https://warrenpowell.org/sdam/>

> Powell, W. B. (2026). *Bridging Decision Problems, Volume I: Framing the Problem*. Kindle Direct Publishing. <https://warrenpowell.org/bridging-vol1/>

## Other uses

If you would like to reproduce a figure, adapt case-study material, translate content, or make other use of the material beyond ordinary citation, please email me at [wbpowell328@gmail.com](mailto:wbpowell328@gmail.com) — I am generally happy to grant permission for non-commercial use.
